Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-08-13

CalmLoop: ASMR Soundboard (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the Android app collects, how it’s used, and your choices.

1) What the app collects

  • No account data: We do not ask for or collect your name, email, or phone number.
  • Crash reporting: We use Firebase Crashlytics to automatically collect crash logs and certain technical data (such as device model, OS version, and app state before the crash) to improve stability and fix bugs. This data is anonymous and does not contain personally identifying information.

2) Permissions

  • INTERNET: Required to stream sounds from archive.org, display ads, and send crash reports to Firebase.

3) Streaming from archive.org

Some sounds in the app are streamed directly from the Internet Archive hosting servers (example: https://dn721808.ca.archive.org/0/items/wind-chimes-sound/wind-chimes-sound.ogg). When you play these items, your device connects directly to archive.org servers to receive the file. The Internet Archive may receive technical data automatically sent by your device, such as your IP address and user-agent, for the purpose of delivering the file. We do not collect this data ourselves, and we do not share or use it for analytics, profiling, or tracking. The Internet Archive’s privacy policy applies to streamed content: https://archive.org/about/privacy.php.

4) Advertising & rewarded ads

The app displays:

  • Banner ads (persistent in the interface)
  • Rewarded ads (to unlock certain sounds)

Ads are provided by a third-party ad network (e.g., Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads). Ad providers may automatically collect identifiers and technical data to measure ad performance, personalize ads (if enabled), and prevent fraud. We do not receive personally identifying information from ad providers.

More info: Advertising Policy  |  Google Privacy Policy

5) Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to help us understand and fix app crashes.

  • Crash logs and stack traces
  • Device model and OS version
  • App version and screen state before crash

Details: Firebase Privacy & Security

6) Children’s privacy

This app is for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the age defined by local law). If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7) Data retention

Crash logs are stored by Firebase for as long as needed to improve app stability.

8) Security

We do not operate backend user databases for this app. Streaming is delivered directly from archive.org hosting servers to your device over HTTPS (if supported by the file URL). Crash reports are transmitted securely to Firebase. No transmission can be guaranteed 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to use secure services.

9) Your choices

  • You can use only offline sounds if you prefer not to stream.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time.

10) Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect functionality or legal changes. The “Last updated” date will be revised, and a new version will be published at the same URL.
